Bo's Kitchen: Baby Food & BLWCreator Program · iPhone

Cook once on Sunday. Feed your baby all week.

Hi, I'm Pavle, a videographer and solo developer from Novi Sad, Serbia. I built Bo's Kitchen for parents starting solids, and I'm looking for a few creators to grow it with. This page explains what the app does, what you get and how you get paid.

What Bo's Kitchen does

  • A week of baby meals, planned for you. Breakfast, lunch and snacks matched to your baby's age, one new food at a time, with an iron-rich food every day. Foods on your avoid list are never suggested.
  • One cooking session for the whole week. One shopping list, a step-by-step cooking order and freezer cubes counted for you, plus a reminder to take tomorrow's cubes out.
  • Recipes in exact grams, and a story of every first bite. Change the number of cubes and every gram updates. Log a meal in seconds and keep a photo of every first. Share the week with a partner through iCloud. No account, no ads.

Free forever: the food guide, allergen and choking guidance, meal logging and the food story. Premium adds the weekly planner, batch cooking, every recipe, reports and partner sharing.

How it works

  1. I send you a code for a free year of Premium. Try it in your own kitchen first. You don't have to post anything.
  2. If you like it, you get two personal codes (yearly and weekly plan) and a personal link for each. The link opens the App Store with your discount already applied.
  3. You put the link in your bio or pinned comment and mention your code in videos. People who already installed the app can type the code under "Have a code?" in the app.
  4. Apple counts every purchase made with your code. You earn on each one, and I send you Apple's numbers every month.

What your followers save, and what you earn.

Yearly code $5 for you, per purchase Your followers pay $19.99 for the first year instead of $29.99.
Weekly code $0.55 for you, per purchase Your followers pay $1.99 a week for the first 4 weeks instead of $2.99.

Prices are in US dollars; Apple shows the local equivalent in other countries. That's roughly 40% of what I receive after Apple's fee. The yearly code is where the money is.

How and when you get paid.

  • Your very first sale is paid right away, as soon as it shows up in App Store Connect.
  • After that, each month is paid by the end of the second month after it. Example: November sales are paid by January 31. That's when Apple has paid me for that month.
  • Payment goes through Payoneer, to your Payoneer account or straight to your bank account. Minimum payout is $20; smaller amounts roll over to the next month.
  • Every month you get a statement: a screenshot of your code's purchases from App Store Connect, your commission, any refund deduction and the amount sent.

Honest limit: for now you see the numbers through my monthly statement, not in your own dashboard. They are Apple's numbers, unchanged.

The fair-play rules

  1. Codes work only for people who have never subscribed to Bo's Kitchen. That's Apple's rule, and it makes every purchase you earn on a real new customer.
  2. Refunds: each month's commission is reduced by that month's refund rate for the same plan, as shown in my App Store financial report. Refunds that arrive later come off the next payout.
  3. Label your posts as sponsored: use the platform's paid-partnership or commercial content setting and add #ad.
  4. No health promises. Don't say the app prevents allergies, picky eating or anything else; it gives general guidance, not medical advice. Keep serving safe on camera, by your baby's age.
  5. Your audience should be adults. Your own baby can of course be in your videos; please don't feature other people's children without their parents' consent. I review your first video before it goes up.
  6. Please don't post your codes on coupon or deal sites, and never offer anything in exchange for App Store ratings or reviews.
  7. Either of us can stop with 14 days' notice. Anything you've already earned is still paid.
